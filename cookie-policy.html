Cookie Policy

This website does not set any cookies, and does not use local storage, session storage, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or other technologies that store or read information on your device. It uses no analytics, advertising or social media trackers, and it does not load any content from third-party domains. For this reason it does not show a cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.

Security cookies set by Cloudflare

All traffic to this website passes through Cloudflare, which protects it from attacks and abuse. Only when its security checks require it (for instance, if your connection looks automated or you are asked to complete a challenge), Cloudflare may set strictly necessary technical cookies, such as:

  • __cf_bm: distinguishes humans from bots, expires after 30 minutes;
  • cf_clearance: records that you passed a security challenge, expires after a short period (30 minutes by default);
  • _cfuvid: used for rate limiting, expires when you close your browser.

These cookies are strictly necessary for the security of the service, so they do not require consent (Art. 122 of Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003 and Art. 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC). More information is available in Cloudflare's cookie documentation.

Other websites

When you submit the contact form you are taken to a page on formspree.io, and when you follow a link to another website (GitHub, LinkedIn, Telegram, WhatsApp, Instagram, the blog, etc.) you leave this website: those pages may use their own cookies, as described in their own cookie policies.

Managing cookies

You can see which cookies are stored on your device, block them and delete them at any time from your browser settings, for instance:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Brave
  • Opera

Blocking Cloudflare's security cookies does not prevent you from browsing this website, but may prevent you from passing its security checks when they are shown.
