Privacy Policy

This page explains which personal data is processed when you visit daniil.it ("this website"), how and why it is processed, and what rights you have, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, "GDPR") and Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003 ("Privacy Code").

In short: this website does not use analytics, advertising, newsletters or profiling, and does not set cookies. Personal data is only processed to deliver and protect the website, and to answer you if you get in touch.

Data controller

Daniil Gentili (VAT 01647560299), Viale Domenico Angeli, 97 - 45100 Rovigo (RO), Italy.
Email: daniil@daniil.it

Types of data processed

  • Browsing data (also called usage data): IP address, requested URL, date and time of the request, HTTP method and response code, size of the response, referring page, browser and operating system (user agent), and approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • Data you provide through the contact form: name, email address, the service you are interested in, the content of your message;
  • Data you provide when contacting the controller directly: your contact details (email address, phone number, or messaging account) and the content of your messages.

This website does not ask for, and you should not send, special categories of personal data (such as health data) through the contact form. If you share personal data of other people through this website, you are responsible for having the right to do so.

Purposes, legal bases and services used

Delivering and protecting the website (hosting, traffic distribution and system logs)

When you open a page your browser sends the browsing data listed above to the servers delivering it. This website is a static site with no server-side code of its own; it is processed by the following services:

  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., United States): all traffic passes through Cloudflare, which acts as a reverse proxy, content delivery network and protection against attacks and abuse (for instance, by blocking malicious requests or asking suspicious visitors to complete a security check). Cloudflare also provides the controller with aggregated traffic statistics (such as number of requests and countries of origin) that do not identify individual visitors. Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies, described in the cookie policy. Privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States): hosts the website files. Privacy statement.
  • Fastly (Fastly, Inc., United States): content delivery network used by GitHub Pages to serve the files. Privacy policy.

These providers may record browsing data in their server logs, for operation, maintenance and security purposes. The controller does not use browsing data to identify visitors or to profile them; it may only be used to investigate attacks or abuse of the website.

Legal basis: the controller's legitimate interest in delivering a fast, secure and available website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Contact form

The contact form collects your name, email address, the service you are interested in and your message. It is sent through Formspree (Formspree, Inc., United States), which receives the submission together with technical data such as your IP address and user agent, filters spam, keeps a copy of the submission in the controller's Formspree account, and forwards it by email to the controller's mailbox. When you submit the form you are taken to a page on formspree.io, which is governed by Formspree's own privacy policy and may ask you to complete a CAPTCHA. Privacy policy.

The controller's mailbox is hosted by Zoho Mail (Zoho Corporation, United States data center). Privacy policy.

Purpose: answering your request and, if you ask for it, preparing a quote or entering into a contract. Legal basis: measures taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for other enquiries, the controller's legitimate interest in replying to the messages it receives (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Your data is not used to send newsletters or marketing communications.

All form fields are required: without them the form cannot be sent, but you can always contact the controller by other means. If you are unsure about which data is required, you can contact the controller.

Direct contacts

If you write to the controller by email, or through the phone, WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, LinkedIn or GitHub links on this website, the controller processes your contact details and the content of your messages in order to reply, for the same purposes and on the same legal bases as the contact form. Those services are operated by third parties under their own privacy policies; this website only links to them and does not load any content from them.

Methods of processing and security

Personal data is processed with IT tools, following procedures strictly related to the purposes described above, and the controller takes appropriate security measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, modification or destruction of the data. Besides the controller, data may be accessed by the service providers listed in this policy, which act as data processors or as independent controllers. Personal data is not sold, and is not disclosed to third parties for their own marketing purposes. An up-to-date list of the providers involved can be requested from the controller at any time.

Place of processing and transfers outside the EU

Data is processed at the controller's offices and wherever the providers listed above are located. Cloudflare and Fastly operate globally distributed networks, so it is not possible to determine in advance which of their servers handles a given request. Since these providers are based in, or may process data in, the United States and other countries outside the European Economic Area, data may be transferred outside the EU. These transfers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for providers certified under it and, otherwise, on the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the European Commission (Articles 45 and 46 GDPR). You can ask the controller for information on the legal basis of these transfers and on the safeguards adopted.

Retention

  • Browsing data: the controller does not keep its own logs; Cloudflare, GitHub and Fastly retain server logs for the limited periods set out in their privacy policies.
  • Contact requests: kept for as long as needed to handle your request and any business relationship that follows it, and then deleted, together with the copy kept by Formspree.
  • Data related to a contract or to invoices is kept for as long as required by law (in Italy, ten years for accounting and tax records).
  • Data may be kept longer when required by a legal obligation or by an order of a public authority, or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Once the retention period expires, personal data is deleted: after that, the rights of access, erasure, rectification and portability can no longer be exercised.

Your rights

To the extent provided by the GDPR (Articles 15-22), you have the right to:

  • access your personal data, and obtain a copy of it and information on how it is processed;
  • have inaccurate data rectified and incomplete data completed;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict the processing of your data, so that it is only stored;
  • receive your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, and have it transmitted to another controller (data portability);
  • object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interest; this website does not process data for direct marketing purposes;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent (no processing on this website is currently based on consent);
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or the authority of the EU country where you live, work, or where the alleged infringement took place.

To exercise your rights, write to daniil@daniil.it. Requests are free of charge and are answered as soon as possible, and in any case within one month (extendable by two further months for complex requests, in which case you will be informed). Any rectification, erasure or restriction is communicated to each recipient the data was disclosed to, unless this proves impossible or involves disproportionate effort; on request, the controller will tell you who these recipients are.

Legal action and requests from authorities

Personal data may be used by the controller to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, including in court, arising from improper use of this website. The controller may be required to disclose personal data upon request of public authorities.

Definitions

  • Personal data: any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • Browsing data (usage data): the technical information automatically collected when a page is requested, listed above.
  • Data subject (user): the natural person the personal data refers to, i.e. you.
  • Data controller: the person who determines the purposes and means of the processing, i.e. Daniil Gentili.
  • Data processor: a person or organization processing personal data on behalf of the controller.
  • Cookie: a small piece of data stored by the browser; more generally, a tracker is any technology that stores or reads information on the user's device.
  • European Union (EU): unless otherwise specified, references to the EU include all member states of the European Economic Area.
